# Reviewing the draft for errors

`check.mjs` catches an invented date, figure or quotation and a missing required element. Everything else is caught by reading the draft the way the district's lawyer and the hearing officer will. Assume there are errors, and look for them against the sources — the pages in `work/text/`, `statement.md`, and the state's pages — never against memory of drafting.

Where you can, give this to a fresh subagent with the case folder and this file.

| Check | Look for |
|---|---|
| **Fidelity** | Every name, date, figure and quotation matches its page — including what a paraphrase implies. Open the pages. Check every caption fact, every filing address and number, and at least a quarter of the numbered paragraphs. Each claim points to the paragraphs that actually bear on it. Where the District's own words are the evidence, they are quoted, not paraphrased. |
| **The state's own rules** | The caption, the respondent's name, any county line, who is served and whether before filing, how the complaint may be sent — all as the receiving office's own page or form says, in `complaint.md` and in `filing-instructions.md`. |
| **Consistency** | Names, defined terms, dates, parties and figures agree across the caption, the body, the relief, the signature block and the certificate. |
| **Posture** | No legal conclusion, advice or outcome language. Each claim's heading fits the facts under it. No paragraph concedes what a claim contests. Events older than the state's time limit are pointed out to the person, without saying whether an exception applies. |
| **Completeness** | Each claim says what the problem is, not only which paragraphs bear on it. The child's name, the address of residence, the school, the problem with its facts, the proposed resolution, anything the state requires, and every remedy the person asked for, each naming its own figures. No blank that should have been filled. |
| **The rendered files** | After rendering, the PDF and Word file read correctly: nothing missing, cut off, reordered or garbled; the caption intact; page numbers; a heading never stranded at the foot of a page. |

Report each error with where it is and what the source actually says. Fix errors at their source (`complaint.md`, `statement.md`, `filing-instructions.md`), not in the rendered file. A judgment that belongs to the person signing is theirs to decide — never settle it yourself.
