# The exemplar — what a finished complaint looks like

This is the standard the skill writes to. Read it before drafting and match its register exactly: **third person** ("Student", "the Parent", "the District"), **nearly every sentence carrying a date, a number, a document, or a specific act**, no legal conclusions in the facts, regulations under the headings only, and **short** — the whole complaint is about 600 words, the statement of the problems under 200. Density is the standard, not length.

The people, district and facts below are invented. Use nothing from it but its shape and voice.

## I. Introduction

Petitioner Jordan R. ("Student") is an eight-year-old student who has been eligible for special education and related services under the category of Autism since March 2024, and who attends Willow Creek Elementary School in the River Oak Unified School District ("District"). This due process complaint is brought on Student's behalf by the parent, Dana R. ("Parent"), who is self-represented.

The District has denied Student a free appropriate public education ("FAPE") by maintaining an unchanged annual reading goal across three consecutive reporting periods during which Student made no measurable progress; by failing to implement the specialized academic instruction the operative individualized education program ("IEP") requires; and by failing to conduct a timely reevaluation after the Parent requested one in writing.

## II. Contact and residence information

*34 C.F.R. § 300.508(b)(1)–(2).*

Student resides with the Parent at 1418 Alder Street, Willow Creek, California 95833, and is enrolled at Willow Creek Elementary School. The Parent may be reached at dana.r@example.com and (555) 010-4471.

## III. Statement of the problems

### A. Failure to revise inadequate annual goals

On September 8, 2025, the IEP team adopted an annual reading goal providing that Student "will increase oral reading fluency to 60 words per minute with 90% accuracy."

Progress reports dated November 14, 2025, January 23, 2026, and March 6, 2026 each recorded oral reading fluency between 21 and 24 words per minute, reflecting no measurable gain across the reporting period.

On March 12, 2026, the IEP team convened and declined to revise the goal. The Parent requested revision in writing on March 14, 2026. The District did not respond.

### B. Failure to implement specialized academic instruction

The September 8, 2025 IEP provides for 240 minutes per week of specialized academic instruction in reading, delivered in a small-group setting by a credentialed special education teacher.

Service logs produced by the District for the period of September 2025 through February 2026 reflect delivery of approximately 95 minutes per week, a shortfall of more than half the minutes the IEP requires.

On March 12, 2026, the District acknowledged that the small-group reading block had been discontinued in October 2025 owing to staffing, and did not offer compensatory services.

## IV. Proposed resolution

*34 C.F.R. § 300.508(b)(6).*

The Parent proposes that the District: (a) convene an IEP team meeting within 15 days to adopt measurable annual reading goals based on current assessment data; (b) fund an independent educational evaluation in reading and written expression; (c) provide compensatory specialized academic instruction equal to the minutes not delivered between October 2025 and February 2026; and (d) implement the IEP as written, with quarterly service logs provided to the Parent.

## What to notice

- **Every paragraph of the statement is a fact with a date or a figure.** "On September 8, 2025, the IEP team adopted…", "between 21 and 24 words per minute", "approximately 95 minutes per week". Nothing is characterised. Note that "a shortfall of more than half" is in words, not a figure: the gate refuses a computed figure ("a shortfall of 145 minutes per week") because no document states it — see `drafting-rules.md`, rule 2. State the figures the documents give and let the reader subtract.
- **The documents are named and dated in the prose** ("Progress reports dated November 14, 2025, January 23, 2026, and March 6, 2026"). There are no page cites in the text; the page lives in sources.md, for the person checking.
- **A quotation is short and exact**, and only where the document's own words are the fact (the goal's wording).
- **Nothing concludes.** "The District did not respond." — not "in violation of its obligations". The regulation under each heading does that work.
- **The introduction is the one place a standard is named**: "denied Student a free appropriate public education ("FAPE") by …" — and the clauses after "by" are the fixed wording of the claims chosen (`references/claims.json`), never composed.
- **The relief is lettered, concrete, and within what a hearing officer can order.**
- **When counsel signs**, the signature block carries the attorney's name, "Attorney for Petitioner", bar number, firm and address, and "Self-represented (pro se)" appears nowhere. The certificate of service is then signed by counsel.
