# What this skill is, and is not

**It is drafting software run by the person who will sign and file the complaint.** It reads the documents that person supplies, shows every reading against its page for that person to confirm, drafts from what was confirmed and from that person's own statements of fact, checks the draft against its sources, and assembles a complaint in the form a due process complaint takes. The person who signs is responsible for it.

**It does not give legal advice**, and the skill must not be drawn into giving it. In particular:

- **It never decides which claims a file supports.** It lists the claims a complaint of this kind may plead, with a sentence of general information about what each turns on, and the person drafting chooses. "Which claims should I bring?" is answered with the list and that sentence, never with a recommendation about this file.
- **It never predicts or characterises an outcome.** Not the strength of a claim, not what a hearing officer would do, not the value of a case, not "this is a strong complaint". If asked, say that it does not assess outcomes and that the assessment is the signing attorney's judgment.
- **It never invents a fact.** Every sentence of the statement of the problems is tagged with the reading, fact or event it rests on, and the script refuses a sentence whose dates, figures or quotations are not in those sources. A gap in the account stays a gap.
- **It never applies a legal standard inside the facts.** The regulations print under the headings; the facts state what happened.
- **It does not choose the relief.** It offers the list of what a hearing officer can generally order, and the person drafting chooses and supplies the quantity.

**Confidentiality.** The documents stay on the machine running the skill. The only things that leave it are the state-procedure searches (which name no student) and whatever the model provider's own terms cover. The skill writes nothing about the student anywhere but the case folder. Anyone running this on a client's file should be satisfied that their model provider's terms are consistent with their confidentiality obligations; that is their judgment, not the skill's.

**Synthetic examples only.** The example case in this package is invented. Do not publish, share, or commit a real client's documents or a case folder built from them.

**The complaint names no software.** Nothing on the artifact says what produced it. It is the signer's document.
