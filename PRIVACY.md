# Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

This policy describes how the Special Education Due Process Complaint Writer handles information when used as a plugin or skill in ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, or another compatible host application.

## What this plugin is

This is a skills-only, open-source plugin. It does not operate a developer-controlled server, user account system, analytics service, advertising service, or database. The plugin developer does not receive or have access to the documents, chat content, or generated files you use with the plugin.

## Information processed

To prepare a due process complaint, the host application may process information you choose to provide, including:

- student and parent or guardian names, contact details, dates, schools, and school districts;
- individualized education programs, evaluations, progress reports, service logs, prior written notices, discipline records, correspondence, and related education records;
- disability, health, accommodation, and service information contained in those records;
- facts you provide in conversation, including the claims and relief you select; and
- drafts, filing instructions, PDF files, and Word files created during the workflow.

This information is processed only to read and organize the records, confirm facts with you, draft and check the complaint, prepare filing instructions, and create the files you request.

## Who receives the information

The documents and chat content you provide are handled by the host application you choose, such as ChatGPT or Claude, under that provider's terms and privacy controls. The plugin developer does not receive them.

The plugin looks up public state filing pages and school-district websites to confirm filing details. Those lookups may identify a state or school district, but the plugin instructs the host application not to include the student's or family's name in a search. Public websites receive ordinary web requests under their own privacy practices.

The plugin does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or disclose it to data brokers.

## Retention

The plugin developer does not collect or retain user documents, chat content, search history, or generated files. The host application may retain chats, uploaded files, code-execution files, and generated outputs according to its terms, account type, workspace settings, and data controls. Files you download remain wherever you save them until you delete them.

## Your choices and controls

You choose which documents and facts to provide. Do not provide records unless you are authorized to use them for this purpose. Parents, guardians, students with transferred education rights, advocates, attorneys, and organizational users are responsible for following the privacy, confidentiality, consent, and professional-responsibility rules that apply to them.

You can stop the workflow, remove attachments where the host application permits, delete chats or downloaded files, and use the host application's privacy and data-control settings. Because school records may contain sensitive information about a child, review the host application's terms and your organization's policies before uploading them.

## Children

The plugin is intended for parents, guardians, adult students, advocates, attorneys, and legal-aid organizations. It is not directed to children under 13.

## Security and limitations

The plugin contains checks designed to catch unsupported dates, figures, and quotations, but no software or AI system is error-free. Review the completed complaint and protect downloaded files appropriately. This plugin does not provide legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.
