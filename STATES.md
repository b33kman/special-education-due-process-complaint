# Where to file a special education due process complaint, by state

For each of the 50 states and the District of Columbia: the office that receives an IDEA special education due process complaint, its official filing page, how to reach it, and the time limit. Compiled from official state pages in August and September 2026.

**Confirm everything on the official filing page before you rely on it.** Offices move, forms change, and a few states’ own pages disagree with each other — where the research found that, the note says so. This page is general information, not legal advice. Where a state sends filings to a named staff member, look up the current contact on the official page; this page lists offices, not people.

**Every state has the complaint go to the school district as well** — federal law requires it (34 C.F.R. § 300.508(a)) — but states differ on which office, and on whether the district gets it first, at the same time, or as a copy. Each state's **The school district** line says which. This page can't list every district's address; the Writer looks up your district's office on the district's own website and prints it, with its address, on the certificate of service.

The [Due Process Complaint Writer](README.md) reads this same information (`skills/due-process-complaint/references/state-rules.json`) and confirms each state’s details on its official filing page on every run.

## Alabama

- **Files with:** Special Education Services, Alabama State Department of Education
- **Official filing page:** <https://www.alabamaachieves.org/special-education/dispute-resolution/>
- **Address:** SES DPH, Special Education Services, Alabama State Department of Education, P.O. Box 30201, Montgomery, AL 36130
- **Email:** dph@alsde.edu — complaints may be filed by email
- **Time limit:** two years from when the parent or agency knew or should have known (34 C.F.R. § 300.507(a)(2)), unless the state’s page says otherwise
- **The school district:** Send a copy to the district superintendent at the same time.

File with the Alabama State Department of Education by mail (SES DPH, P.O. Box 30201, Montgomery) or by email to dph@alsde.edu. At the same time, send a copy to the superintendent of the school district or public agency serving the child; the sample form is optional. The complaint must concern a violation that occurred not more than two years before the parent or agency knew or should have known about it.

Complete three copies of form PI-2115 (or write a letter): keep one, give one to the school district, and send the signed original to the Director, Special Education Team, Wisconsin Department of Public Instruction, PO Box 7841, or by fax ((608) 267-3746) or email (IDEA@DPI.WI.GOV). Requests submitted outside business hours are marked received the next business day. Wisconsin's window is one year: the request must be filed within one year after the district refused or proposed to initiate or change the child's evaluation, individualized education program, educational placement, or provision of a free appropriate public education.

## Wyoming

- **Files with:** Wyoming Department of Education, Special Education Programs Division
- **Official filing page:** <https://edu.wyoming.gov/parents/special-education/dispute-resolution/>
- **Address:** State Director of Special Education, WDE Special Education Programs Division, 122 W. 25th St. Suite E200,, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82002
- **Email:** wde-disputeinbox@wyo.gov — the official pages do not say whether complaints may be filed by email
- **Fax:** (307) 777-6234 — complaints may be filed by fax
- **Time limit:** two years from when the parent or agency knew or should have known (34 C.F.R. § 300.507(a)(2)), unless the state’s page says otherwise
- **The school district:** Send a copy to the school district or public agency responsible for the child at the same time.

File the request with the Wyoming Department of Education by mail or fax ((307) 777-6234) to the State Director of Special Education, and send a copy to the school district or public agency responsible for the child. The dispute inbox (wde-disputeinbox@wyo.gov) is printed on the form, but filing by email is not stated on the official pages researched. The request must allege a violation that happened not more than two years before the parent or district knew or should have known about it; the model form is optional.
