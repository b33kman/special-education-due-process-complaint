## How to file in North Carolina

Verified on the web on 2026-09-14; every line below cites a page listed in sources.md.

- **Where it goes:** The ORIGINAL petition is filed with the Office of Administrative Hearings (the case commences when it is received and filed there). A COPY must be mailed or hand-delivered to the Superintendent of the board of education named as respondent, and a copy mailed or faxed to the N.C. Department of Public Instruction — the person designated by the State Board under G.S. 115C-107.2(b)(9), who is served simultaneously with the petition.
- **Address:** Office of Administrative Hearings, 1711 New Hope Church Road, Raleigh, NC 27609 (mailing and physical address). Copy to the N. C. Department of Public Instruction, 6356 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-6356 (or by fax to (919) 807-3243).
- **How it may be sent:** Mail or hand delivery of the original to 1711 New Hope Church Road, Raleigh, NC 27609. Fax to the OAH Clerk's Office at (984) 236-1871 during normal business hours (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday–Friday). Email to oah.clerks@oah.nc.gov with the petition attached as a PDF or Word-compatible file — 'Electronic mail without attached file shall not constitute a valid filing'; OAH's FAQ adds that a fax or email filing is deemed filed provided the original signed document is received within seven business days. Registered e-filers upload a Word or PDF to the OAH e-filing portal and may not file by fax or email. The DPI copy goes by mail to 6356 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-6356 or by fax to (919) 807-3243.
- **Form:** No form is required: OAH states that a petition need not be filed on the provided form, but that using it ensures all necessary information is included. The provided form is OAH Form H-06E (rev. 06/21), 'Petition for a Contested Case Hearing (Special Education)', at https://www.oah.nc.gov/form-h-06e-special-education-petition-form (file: https://www.oah.nc.gov/documents/files/h-06e/open). Manifestation determination cases use Form H-06E-A instead.
- **Serve:** the Superintendent of Pine Hollow County Schools; the North Carolina Department of Public Instruction, 6356 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-6356
- **Window (months):** 12
- **Required beyond § 300.508(b):** the county of the student's residence — the caption's 'COUNTY OF' line and the county on the home-address line (7) of Form H-06E; OAH assigns the hearing region from the county on the petition; the student's birthdate (Form H-06E line (6)); the basis of the petition, checked from the form's categories: identification, evaluation, educational placement, denial of a free appropriate public education, other (Form H-06E section (8)); the petitioner's telephone number and full address (Form H-06E lines (12)–(13)); a certificate of service naming the superintendent served and the school board's address (Form H-06E lines (16)–(20)); the petition must also state the information required under IDEA (G.S. 115C-109.6(b))
- **Notes:** CAPTION AND RESPONDENT: Form H-06E captions the case 'STATE OF NORTH CAROLINA / IN THE OFFICE OF ADMINISTRATIVE HEARINGS / COUNTY OF ___ / ___ EDC ___' and names the respondent as the '___ Board of Education' (line 3). LIMITATIONS: G.S. 115C-109.6(b) allows a petition to set forth a violation that occurred not more than one year before the party knew or reasonably should have known of it (a discovery rule); subsection (c) states two exceptions (misrepresentation that the problem was resolved; withholding of information the LEA was required to provide). Whether the rule or an exception reaches any event is counsel's judgment. OAH's FAQ states a general 60-day limit under G.S. 150B-23(f) for contested cases and says another statute may set the limit; for special education petitions G.S. 115C-109.6(b) does.
