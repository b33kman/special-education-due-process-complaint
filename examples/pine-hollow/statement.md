# Counsel’s statement

In their own words, as given. This file counts as a source for the complaint.

## What the district did, or failed to do

the IEP of August 21, 2025 requires 60 minutes per week of individual speech-language therapy; from the week of September 22, 2025 the district delivered 30 minutes per week in a group of three, with no service in three weeks, for a total of 690 minutes against 1,320 required through January 30, 2026, and did not amend the IEP; the parent requested an independent educational evaluation in writing on November 10, 2025 and the district refused it on December 4, 2025 without filing for a hearing; the district's May 6, 2025 evaluation did not assess written expression.

## How the Parent learned of it

from the service log the district sent on January 21, 2026, from the prior written notice of December 4, 2025, and from the student's own reports.

## Effect on the student

her expressive language remains at the 2nd percentile per the May 2025 CELF-5 and she has become reluctant to speak in class, per the IEP's present levels.

## Relief sought

compensatory speech-language therapy, delivered individually, equal to the individual therapy minutes the IEP required and the District did not deliver individually from September 22, 2025 to the date of the order; an independent educational evaluation in speech-language and written expression at public expense; and restoration of individual speech-language therapy at 60 minutes per week as the IEP requires.
