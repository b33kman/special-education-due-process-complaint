# How to file in North Carolina

Checked on 2026-09-14 against the official pages cited under each point.

## Filed with

The ORIGINAL petition is filed with the Office of Administrative Hearings (the case commences when it is received and filed there). A COPY must be mailed or hand-delivered to the Superintendent of the board of education named as respondent, and a copy mailed or faxed to the N.C. Department of Public Instruction — the person designated by the State Board under G.S. 115C-107.2(b)(9), who is served simultaneously with the petition.

Source: <https://www.oah.nc.gov/documents/files/h-06e/open>, <https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_115C/GS_115C-109.6.html>, <https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_115C/GS_115C-107.2.html>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/hearings-division/hearing-process/filing-contested-case>, <https://www.dpi.nc.gov/documents/publications/catalog/ec145-procedural-safeguards/open>

## Filing address

Office of Administrative Hearings, 1711 New Hope Church Road, Raleigh, NC 27609 (mailing and physical address). Copy to the N. C. Department of Public Instruction, 6356 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-6356, or by fax to (919) 807-3243.

Source: <https://www.oah.nc.gov/documents/files/h-06e/open>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/form-h-06e-special-education-petition-form>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/contact>

## How it may be sent

Mail or hand delivery of the original to 1711 New Hope Church Road, Raleigh, NC 27609. Fax to the OAH Clerk's Office at (984) 236-1871 during normal business hours (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday–Friday). Email to oah.clerks@oah.nc.gov with the petition attached as a PDF or Word-compatible file — 'Electronic mail without attached file shall not constitute a valid filing'; OAH's FAQ adds that a fax or email filing is deemed filed provided the original signed document and the filing fee (where required) are received within seven business days. Registered e-filers upload a Word or PDF to the OAH e-filing portal and may not file by fax or email. The DPI copy goes by mail to 6356 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-6356 or by fax to (919) 807-3243.

Source: <https://www.oah.nc.gov/documents/files/h-06e/open>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/hearings-division/hearing-process/filing-contested-case>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/frequently-asked-questions-about-hearing-process>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/contact>

## State form

No form is required: OAH states that a petition need not be filed on the provided form, but that using it ensures all necessary information is included. The provided form is OAH Form H-06E (rev. 06/21), 'Petition for a Contested Case Hearing (Special Education)', at https://www.oah.nc.gov/form-h-06e-special-education-petition-form (file: https://www.oah.nc.gov/documents/files/h-06e/open). Manifestation determination cases use Form H-06E-A instead.

Source: <https://www.oah.nc.gov/documents/files/h-06e/open>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/form-h-06e-special-education-petition-form>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/hearings-division/hearing-process/filing-contested-case>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/frequently-asked-questions-about-hearing-process>

## Required beyond § 300.508(b)

the county of the student's residence — the caption's 'COUNTY OF' line and the county on the home-address line (7) of Form H-06E; OAH assigns the hearing region from the county on the petition; the student's birthdate (Form H-06E line (6)); the basis of the petition, checked from the form's categories: identification, evaluation, educational placement, denial of a free appropriate public education, other (Form H-06E section (8)); the petitioner's telephone number and full address (Form H-06E lines (12)–(13)); a certificate of service naming the superintendent served (Form H-06E's certificate of service); the petition must also state the information required under IDEA (G.S. 115C-109.6(b))

Source: <https://www.oah.nc.gov/documents/files/h-06e/open>, <https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_115C/GS_115C-109.6.html>, <https://www.oah.nc.gov/frequently-asked-questions-about-hearing-process>, <https://www.dpi.nc.gov/documents/publications/catalog/ec145-procedural-safeguards/open>

## Where official pages disagree: the mailing address for the copy to the Department of Public Instruction

Form H-06E’s instructions (S1), written for this filing, direct the copy to 6356 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-6356; DPI’s dispute-resolution page (S5) gives the department’s general mailing address as 6301 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-6301. The form is followed; confirm the designated person’s address with DPI’s Exceptional Children Division, (919) 807-3969, before filing.
