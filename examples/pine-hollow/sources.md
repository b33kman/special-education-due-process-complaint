## Counsel’s statements of fact

- **F1** — *What the district did, or failed to do*: the IEP of August 21, 2025 requires 60 minutes per week of individual speech-language therapy; from the week of September 22, 2025 the district delivered 30 minutes per week in a group of three, with no service in three weeks, for a total of 690 minutes against 1,320 required through January 30, 2026, and did not amend the IEP; the parent requested an independent educational evaluation in writing on November 10, 2025 and the district refused it on December 4, 2025 without filing for a hearing; the district's May 6, 2025 evaluation did not assess written expression.
- **F2** — *How the Parent learned of it*: from the service log the district sent on January 21, 2026, from the prior written notice of December 4, 2025, and from the student's own reports.
- **F3** — *Effect on the student*: her expressive language remains at the 2nd percentile per the May 2025 CELF-5 and she has become reluctant to speak in class, per the IEP's present levels.
- **F4** — *Relief sought*: compensatory speech-language therapy, delivered individually, equal to the minutes not delivered from September 22, 2025 to the date of the order; an independent educational evaluation in speech-language and written expression at public expense; and restoration of individual speech-language therapy at 60 minutes per week as the IEP requires.
