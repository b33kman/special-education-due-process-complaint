Special Education Division

As part of the Office of Administrative Hearings, provides fair and neutral resolutions for special education disputes.  Click Contact Information for  Case Manager contact information.

Access Self-Help Tools, Tips, and Guides for the Special Education Due Process Matters

Visit the Information page for helpful guides, tools, and tips for understanding and navigating Special Education due process hearings and mediation.

Advisory Committee Meeting Information

Access the latest information regarding the Special Education Advisory Committee, including upcoming meeting dates, documents, and location details.

Find a Special Education Form Including Requests for Hearing or Mediation

Already know what form you need? Go directly to the Special Educaton Forms page. If you are unsure what forms to use, visit the Self-Help pages for more understanding of due process procedures - just click on the Self-Help tab at the top of the page.

Contact Your Case Manager

Find Special Education Case Managers' contact information. To identify the Case Manager assigned to your matter please review your Scheduling Order.

Special Education Surveys for Mediation and Hearing Participants

If you recently participated in a Special Education due process hearing or mediation, you may have a link to a survey to provide feedback using our online surveys. This link will take you to the Survey page for more information on completing your survey.

Reduced Cost or Free Special Education Attorneys and Advocates Lists (Self-Certified)

OAH does not provide legal advice. However, we do provide lists of attorneys and advocates who specialize in assisting with special education cases and have self-certified themselves as low-cost and/or free.
