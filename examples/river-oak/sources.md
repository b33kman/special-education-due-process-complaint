## The Parent’s statements of fact

- **F1** — *What the district did, or failed to do*: The IEP team kept the same reading fluency goal on September 8, 2025 that it had set on March 11, 2024, and refused on March 12, 2026 to change it even though Jordan's fluency was 21, 24 and 23 words per minute on the three progress reports. The district stopped the small-group reading block on October 6, 2025 and delivered about 95 minutes a week of the 240 minutes the IEP requires through February 27, 2026. I asked in writing on December 2, 2025 for a reevaluation of Jordan's reading and have not received an assessment plan.
- **F2** — *How the client learned of it*: From the progress reports dated November 14, 2025, January 23, 2026 and March 6, 2026; from the service delivery log the district gave me for September 8, 2025 to February 27, 2026; from the prior written notice dated March 12, 2026; and from Mr. Ellison's email of December 4, 2025.
- **F3** — *Effect on the student*: Jordan read 22 words per minute in August 2025 and 23 words per minute in March 2026. The IEP says the end-of-second-grade benchmark is 87 words per minute. He is in third grade.
- **F4** — *Relief sought*: An IEP meeting within 15 days to adopt measurable reading goals based on current assessment data; an independent educational evaluation in reading and written expression at the district's expense; compensatory specialized academic instruction equal to the minutes not delivered from October 6, 2025 through February 27, 2026; and the IEP implemented as written, with quarterly service logs sent to me.
