# How to file in California

Checked on 2026-09-14 against the official pages cited under each point.

## Filed with

The request is filed with the Office of Administrative Hearings, Special Education Division, which conducts the hearing under the supervision of the California Department of Education. A copy goes to the school district at the same time, and a Proof of Service showing that it did is filed with the request: OAH accepts a document for filing only after it has been served on the other party.

Source: <https://www.dgs.ca.gov/OAH/Case-Types/Special-Education/Services/Page-Content/Special-Education-Services-List-Folder/Request-Special-Education-Due-Process-Hearing>, <https://www.dgs.ca.gov/OAH/Case-Types/Special-Education/Resources/Page-Content/Special-Education-Resources-List-Folder/Notice-of-Procedural-Safeguards>, <https://california.public.law/codes/education_code_section_56502>, <https://www.dgs.ca.gov/OAH/Case-Types/Special-Education/Self-Help/Filing-and-Serving-Documents>

## The school district

The complaint is served on the Superintendent, River Oak Unified School District, 500 Oak Valley Road, Willow Creek, CA 95833, telephone (555) 010-3000 — before it is filed with the Office of Administrative Hearings, as above. California's rules name no particular office within the district, so it goes to the superintendent's office.

Source: This example's district is invented, so its office has no website. In a real case the office and address come from the district's own official website, checked against the letterhead on the district's documents.

## How it may be sent

OAH’s Secure e-File Transfer (SFT) system; U.S. mail, hand delivery, or overnight delivery to the Sacramento address. The SFT does not serve other parties: the district is served separately, before filing.

Source: <https://www.dgs.ca.gov/OAH/Case-Types/Special-Education/Self-Help/Filing-and-Serving-Documents>, <https://www.dgs.ca.gov/oah/case-types/special-education>

## Where official pages disagree: whether a due process complaint may be sent by fax

OAH’s page on filing and serving documents (S7) lists the Secure e-File Transfer system, mail, hand delivery and overnight delivery, and no fax. OAH’s procedural safeguards notice (S2) and the Department of Education’s dispute-resolution page (S3) each still give a fax number, and not the same one. The office’s own filing instructions are followed; a filer who wants to fax should confirm with OAH first.

Sources: <https://www.dgs.ca.gov/OAH/Case-Types/Special-Education/Resources/Page-Content/Special-Education-Resources-List-Folder/Notice-of-Procedural-Safeguards>, <https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/disputeresolution.asp>, <https://www.dgs.ca.gov/OAH/Case-Types/Special-Education/Self-Help/Filing-and-Serving-Documents>

## Where official pages disagree: when the district is served

Education Code § 56502(c)(1) (S4) requires the copy to the other party at the same time as the request is filed; OAH’s filing page (S7) accepts a document only once the other parties have been served. Serving the district first and filing with the proof of service meets both.

Sources: <https://california.public.law/codes/education_code_section_56502>, <https://www.dgs.ca.gov/OAH/Case-Types/Special-Education/Self-Help/Filing-and-Serving-Documents>
